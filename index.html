What is Pitch Changer?

Pitch Changer lets you change the key of songs on any website and pitch-shift and transpose music on your computer without complicated audio software. It is built for real-world use like practicing, teaching, recording, and simply having fun with your favorite tracks.

You can:

  • Change song key up or down in semitones.
  • Practice in the key that fits your voice or instrument.
  • Prepare backing tracks, karaoke versions and teaching materials.
  • Experiment with arrangements, harmonies and creative pitch shifts.

Pitch Changer comes in two versions: Pitch Changer Extension a browser extension for websites, and Pitch Changer App an iPhone, iPad and Mac app for files.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between Pitch Changer Extension and Pitch Changer App?

Pitch Changer Extension is a browser extension that changes the key of songs directly on websites like YouTube and other audio or video platforms.

Pitch Changer App is a standalone iPhone, iPad and Mac app that works offline with your own audio files and can batch process multiple tracks at once.

Do I need an internet connection to use Pitch Changer?

The Pitch Changer extension runs in your browser and is designed for online content, so you will typically use it while connected to the internet.

The iPhone, iPad and Mac app works fully offline with local audio files, so once it is installed you can use it without an active connection.

Which audio formats does Pitch Changer App support?

The iPhone, iPad and Mac app works with common audio formats such as MP3, WAV, FLAC and more, so you can process the files you already have without converting them first.

You can also add videos, regions from your DAW and whole folders. Pitch Changer extracts the audio for you.

Will changing pitch affect audio quality?

Pitch Changer is designed to preserve audio quality while you adjust the key. Our users regularly say the pitch shift quality is excellent.

As with any digital pitch shifting, very extreme changes may sound less natural, but in most cases the result stays clean and musical.

I can't find instrumental versions of songs. Can you help?

Check out our Vocal Remover. It removes vocals from a song online or offline and works beautifully with Pitch Changer.

How can I change the tempo of a song?

If you want to slow down or speed up music on a website while it is already playing, use BPM Changer.

Is there a free trial? How does pricing work?
