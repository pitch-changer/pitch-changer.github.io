D is two semitones above C. Set the slider to +2 to turn C G/B Am7 F into D A/C# Bm7 G with sharps selected. The bass note in G/B moves up two semitones, too.

Frequently asked questions

Can I paste chords together with lyrics?

Yes. Put chords on their own lines or use brackets for chords within a lyric line, as in “[C]Sing it [G]again.” Keep section headings and other notes on separate lines.

What happens to minor and seventh chords?

The chord quality stays the same. For example, moving the slider to +2 turns Am7 into Bm7 and D7 into E7.

Why do the chords look the same at +12 or −12?

Twelve semitones make an octave. The same chord names apply in either octave, so the chart keeps its original spelling.
