How to change a video's pitch

A video pitch changer raises or lowers the sound in a video while keeping the tempo steady. Use it to put a song in a key that fits your voice or instrument.

  1. Select "Choose video file" or drop a video onto the tool.
  2. Press Play and adjust Pitch in semitones as you listen. Negative values lower the sound; positive values raise it. You can shift from −12 to +12 semitones.
  3. Use Reset pitch to compare with the original. Then return to the setting you want.

Save audio for rehearsal

Select Download audio once the song is in the right key. You can practice with the saved track on your own or send it to your group before rehearsal.

Match the key you’ll rehearse in

If your rehearsal video is in E-flat major and your group plays in D major, set the pitch to −1 semitone. Play along with the chorus to check the fit before saving.

Frequently asked questions

Can I export the video with the new pitch?

The export contains only the pitch-shifted audio.

What if my video won’t open?

Try another browser or a different copy of the video.

Can I save audio from a long video?

Yes. A full rehearsal or performance may take longer to prepare. If the download fails, try another browser.

Does my video get uploaded?

All processing happens locally in your browser. Your video file never leaves your device.

Can I paste a YouTube link?

This tool opens video files. To change the pitch of videos playing on YouTube and other websites, use Pitch Changer Extension.
