How to change a video's pitch

A video pitch changer raises or lowers the sound in a video while keeping the tempo steady. Use it to put a song in a key that fits your voice or instrument.

  1. Select "Choose video file" or drop a video onto the tool.
  2. Press Play and adjust Pitch in semitones as you listen. Negative values lower the sound; positive values raise it. You can shift from −12 to +12 semitones.
  3. Use Reset pitch to compare with the original. Then return to the setting you want.
  4. Select Download audio to save the shifted sound.

Try a lower key

If a backing track is in D major and feels too high, set the pitch to −2 semitones to hear it in C major. The tempo stays the same. Voices and instruments shift together.

Frequently asked questions

Can I download the changed video?

The download contains the shifted audio only. It does not create a new video file.

Which video files can I use?

Try an MP4 or WebM file. Compatibility depends on how the video's picture and sound are encoded and what your browser supports.

Can I use a video longer than 10 minutes?

You can play longer videos and change their pitch. Audio downloads are limited to videos up to 10 minutes long.

Can I paste a YouTube link?

This tool opens video files. To change the pitch of videos playing on YouTube and other websites, use Pitch Changer Extension.
