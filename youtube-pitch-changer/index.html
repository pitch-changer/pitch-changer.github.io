How to change pitch on YouTube

  1. Add Pitch Changer from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Open the YouTube video you want to practice with and press Play.
  3. Open Pitch Changer from Chrome’s toolbar or Extensions menu. Move Pitch Change (semitones) left to lower the key or right to raise it. Select Reset to hear the original key.

Find a key that fits

If the highest note in a chorus is A and you’d rather sing it as G, lower the pitch by two semitones. Then sing through the chorus again at the same tempo.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use my own YouTube link in the demo?

The demo plays a sample clip. To change the pitch of another video, open it on YouTube and use Pitch Changer Extension while it plays.

Can I change just the singer’s pitch?

Pitch Changer shifts the whole mix. The voice and instruments move by the same interval.

Does the pitch change affect the upload?

You hear the change during playback. The original upload stays as it is.

What if I already have a video file?

Open it in Video pitch changer to watch it in a different key and save the shifted audio.
