How to change pitch on YouTube

  1. 1. Add the extension (Extension is installed)

    Add to Chrome

    In the Chrome Web Store, select Add to Chrome, then Add extension.

  2. 2.Open YouTube

    Open the YouTube video you want to practice with and press Play.

  3. 3.Change the pitch

    Pitch Change (semitones):
    +2
    Reset

    Open Pitch Changer from the browser toolbar or Extensions menu. Move the slider left to lower the key or right to raise it.

Find a key that fits

If the chorus tops out at C and you want that note to land on A, set the pitch to −3 in the YouTube tab. Sing through the whole chorus. If the low notes are hard to reach, try −2.

Frequently asked questions

Can I change just the singer’s pitch?

Pitch Changer shifts the whole mix. The voice and instruments move by the same interval.

Does the pitch change affect the upload?

You hear the change during playback. The original upload stays as it is.

What if I already have a video file?

Open it in Video pitch changer to watch it in a different key and save the shifted audio.
